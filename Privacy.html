Privacy Policy

App: Sitaram (Faceless Bot)   Last updated: November 2026

1. What we access

When you connect your Google account, the app asks for one permission: upload videos to your YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload). It is used only to upload videos that you asked the app to create, to your own channel.

2. Where data is stored

The app has no server. Your settings, API keys and the Google login token are stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.

3. Third-party services

To create a video, the app sends the topic and script text to Google (Gemini) and, if you enable them, to image providers (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Pollinations). Videos are uploaded to YouTube. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete all local data from Android Settings > Apps > Faceless Bot > Storage > Clear data.

6. Contact

Questions: please contact the developer using the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
