Sitaram

Sitaram is a personal assistant app that helps a creator write short video scripts with AI, create short vertical videos, and upload them to the creator's own YouTube channel on a schedule the creator chooses.

What it does

  • Writes a short script for a topic chosen by the user
  • Creates narration, adds images and builds a short video on the user's phone
  • Uploads the finished video to the user's own YouTube channel, only after the user connects their Google account

Your data

The app runs on the user's device. Settings, API keys and the YouTube login token stay on that device. See the Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
